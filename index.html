// What I Do

Services

From routine maintenance to full engine diagnostics — I handle it all with the same care. I work around your schedule and come to you, or you can drop the car at my private workshop.

01 —

Routine Maintenance

Oil changes, fluid top-ups, filter replacements, spark plugs, belts, and full service intervals. Keeping your car healthy and your warranty intact.

02 —

Diagnostics

OBD-II scanning, fault code analysis, and proper root-cause diagnosis. No guesswork, no unnecessary part replacements.

03 —

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, discs, callipers, wheel bearings, shock absorbers, springs, and geometry checks. Safety-critical work done with zero shortcuts.

04 —

Engine & Gearbox

Timing chain/belt jobs, head gaskets, clutch replacements, and gearbox rebuilds. Complex work explained in plain English before we touch anything.

05 —

Electrical & AC

Battery, alternator, starter motor, wiring faults, and full air conditioning service including regas and leak detection.

06 —

MOT Prep & Pre-Buy

Full pre-MOT inspection to catch failures before the day. Pre-purchase inspections so you know exactly what you're buying.
